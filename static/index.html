Privacy & Electronic Signature Policy

1. Sole Identity Verification ("Sign Only To Your Name")

By accessing this system, you agree that your digital signature constitutes a legally binding execution exclusively under your authenticated name. Signing documents under another individual's name or on fields assigned to another recipient is strictly prohibited under 18 U.S.C. § 1028 and the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act.

2. Cryptographic Integrity & Tamper-Evidence

All signed documents are sealed with an irreversible SHA-256 cryptographic digest. Any alteration of the document post-execution invalidates the certificate seal.

3. Immutable Audit Trail

Audit logs including IP addresses, authenticated timestamps, and electronic signature disclosures are embedded in the final Certificate of Completion.
